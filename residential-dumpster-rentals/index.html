Disposal Guidelines

What Can You Put In A Residential Dumpster?

Keep your project safe and compliant. Review our complete guide to accepted debris and restricted items before loading your container.

✔ Accepted Household Waste

  • Couches, sofas, mattresses, tables, dressers & chairs
  • Drywall, cabinets, flooring, carpet & bathroom fixtures
  • Roofing shingles, deck lumber & siding
  • Yard debris, tree branches, brush & sod
  • General household clutter, boxes & toys

✖ Prohibited Items

  • Wet paint cans, lacquers & thinners
  • Car batteries & automotive fluids
  • Asbestos & hazardous waste
  • Propane tanks & compressed gas
  • Refrigerators & AC units (freon)
