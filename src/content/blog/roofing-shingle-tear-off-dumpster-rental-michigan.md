Replacing a roof in Metro Detroit is one of the most demanding home improvement projects. Between Michigan's heavy freeze-thaw winter cycles and severe summer storms, roofs across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties take a beating—leading thousands of homeowners to tear off old shingles every season.

However, many homeowners and first-time roofing contractors make a critical mistake during tear-off: **underestimating how much roofing shingles weigh**.

Because asphalt shingles are dense and packed with petroleum and granules, they weigh far more per cubic yard than general household junk. Renting the wrong size dumpster can lead to costly weight overload fees at local landfills.

Here is **Dumpsters Now's** field guide to selecting the perfect roll-off container for your Michigan roofing project.

### Understanding Roofing Terminology: What is a "Square"?

In the roofing industry, materials are measured in **squares**:
- **1 Square** = 100 square feet of roof surface (equivalent to 3 bundles of standard asphalt shingles).
- The average single-family home in Metro Detroit (such as a ranch in Livonia or a colonial in Canton) has between **20 and 30 squares** of roofing area.

#### Shingle Weight Estimates:
- **3-Tab Asphalt Shingles**: Weigh approximately **200 to 250 lbs per square** (single layer).
- **Architectural / Dimensional Shingles**: Weigh approximately **350 to 450 lbs per square** (single layer).
- **Multiple Layers**: If your roof has two layers of old shingles to tear off, double those weight calculations!
