## How to Calculate Roofing Shingle Weight

Roofing is one project where weight calculations matter substantially.

A useful estimating figure for standard **3-tab asphalt shingles is approximately 230 pounds per roofing square**. One roofing square covers roughly **100 square feet** of roof area.

Use this formula:

**Number of roofing squares × 230 pounds = estimated shingle weight**

Then:

**Estimated pounds ÷ 2,000 = estimated tons**

For example, suppose a Northville roofing contractor removes 25 squares:

**25 × 230 = 5,750 pounds**

**5,750 ÷ 2,000 = 2.875 tons**

That means the removed 3-tab shingles alone weigh approximately **2.88 tons**, before accounting for underlayment, damaged decking, nails, flashing, packaging, or other material.

Multiple roofing layers increase the amount significantly. Architectural shingles can also be heavier than the 230-pound 3-tab reference figure, so the actual roofing material should always be considered.

## Preparing Your Northville Driveway for Dumpster Delivery

Good preparation makes delivery substantially easier.

First, select a level location with enough room for the dumpster and delivery truck. Remove cars, trailers, basketball equipment, trash cans, and other obstacles before the scheduled delivery.

Look upward as well. Low tree branches, wires, and similar obstructions can interfere with roll-off delivery equipment.

You should also consider whether you'll need access to your garage while the dumpster is present. Placing a container directly in front of a garage door can make sense for a rapid cleanout but can become frustrating during a longer renovation.
