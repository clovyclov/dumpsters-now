## Roofing Dumpster Calculations for Northville Homes

Roofing debris deserves special attention because shingles become heavy very quickly.

A useful estimating figure is:

**1 roofing square of 3-tab asphalt shingles ≈ 230 pounds**

A roofing "square" means **100 square feet of roof area**, not a 100-square-foot pile of waste.

For example, suppose a Northville roof contains 25 squares of 3-tab shingles:

**25 squares × 230 pounds = 5,750 pounds**

Because one ton equals 2,000 pounds:

**5,750 ÷ 2,000 = 2.875 tons**

That estimate is for the shingles themselves. Underlayment, nails, flashing, damaged decking, multiple roofing layers, and other debris can add substantial weight.

For a roof with multiple shingle layers, do not calculate solely from the home's square footage. A 25-square roof with two tear-off layers can produce much more debris than a single-layer replacement.

Providing the roof size, number of layers, and roofing material when you order helps us recommend a suitable dumpster.

## How to Avoid Dumpster Overweight Charges

Estimating weight before delivery is one of the easiest ways to keep a project predictable.

Use this simple formula:

**Estimated tons = total debris pounds ÷ 2,000**

For roofing shingles:

**Estimated pounds = number of roofing squares × 230 pounds per square**

Then account for additional material, including other roof layers and decking.

For mixed household cleanouts, exact weight calculations are more difficult. Describe the project when booking instead. A dumpster filled primarily with couches, boxes, and household clutter behaves very differently from one loaded with tile, plaster, shingles, or masonry.

Also keep debris within the allowable fill level. Material should not protrude dangerously above the container walls when the dumpster is transported.
