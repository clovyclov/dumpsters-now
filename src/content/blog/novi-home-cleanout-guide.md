## How to Estimate the Right Dumpster Size for Your Novi Cleanout

Choosing the right container can save money and reduce unnecessary delays.

Here's a straightforward estimating method.

### Step 1: Estimate Your Debris Volume

Measure the approximate length, width, and height of your junk pile in feet.

Then use this formula:

**Length × Width × Height ÷ 27 = Cubic Yards**

For example, suppose you have a pile measuring:

- 12 feet long
- 8 feet wide
- 4 feet high

The calculation is:

**12 × 8 × 4 = 384 cubic feet**

**384 ÷ 27 = approximately 14.2 cubic yards**

That suggests a 15-yard dumpster might accommodate the pile if the material can be loaded efficiently.

However, irregularly shaped furniture and loading gaps may make a 20-yard container a safer choice.

### Step 2: Account for Material Weight

Volume isn't the only factor.

A pile of cardboard boxes weighs much less than the same volume of tile, concrete, or plaster.

For household cleanouts, consider whether your debris consists mostly of lightweight possessions or heavy renovation material.

### Step 3: Allow Room for Unexpected Junk

During estate cleanouts and basement projects, homeowners frequently uncover more belongings than anticipated.

If your estimated volume is close to a dumpster's maximum capacity, consider sizing up.

For example, moving from a 20-yard dumpster at $425 to a 30-yard dumpster at $465 represents a **$40 difference in starting price**.

That provides an additional 10 cubic yards of capacity and another ton of included disposal weight.

For a substantial cleanout, the extra room may be worthwhile.
