## Estimating Debris Weight for Plymouth Kitchen & Bath Projects

Demolition waste from kitchens and bathrooms is significantly heavier than general household clutter because of high-density materials:

### 1. Cabinetry & Countertops
- **Laminate & Wood Cabinets:** A standard 10-foot kitchen cabinet run weighs approximately **250 – 400 lbs**.
- **Granite & Quartz Countertops:** Heavy stone countertops weigh roughly **18 – 25 lbs per square foot**. A typical 50 sq. ft. kitchen counter section can weigh over **1,000 lbs**.

### 2. Tile, Drywall & Plaster (Historic Plymouth Homes)
- Older homes near Downtown Plymouth and Old Village frequently feature lathe and plaster walls, which weigh up to **8 – 10 lbs per square foot** (nearly double modern drywall at 1.6 lbs/sq. ft.).
- Floor and wall ceramic/porcelain tile weighs between **4 – 6 lbs per square foot**. A typical 80 sq. ft. bathroom floor and shower tear-out yields over **500 lbs** of heavy rubble.
