Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Dumpsters Now ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you access our website at dumpstersnow.com, request a roll-off dumpster quote, or book waste management services online across Metro Detroit, Michigan.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal and technical information when you interact with our website, request quotes, or utilize our booking tools, including:

  • Contact Details: Your name, telephone number, email address, and service delivery address submitted through quote forms, booking portals, or phone consultations.
  • Rental & Job Specifications: Requested container size (15, 20, 30, or 40-yard), desired delivery date, placement instructions (e.g., driveway or street), and debris category (residential, construction, junk, or roofing materials).
  • Automated & Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral sources, pages viewed, time spent on site, and interaction data captured via cookies, Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for legitimate operational and business purposes, including:

  • Processing dumpster rental orders, confirming drop-off schedules, and managing roll-off pickup logistics.
  • Sending order confirmations, driver ETA updates, delivery notifications, and invoices via phone, SMS, or email.
  • Providing timely customer support, answering inquiries, and coordinating driveway placement details.
  • Analyzing web traffic, monitoring site security, preventing fraudulent activity, and improving user experience.

4. SMS & Mobile Communications Policy

By providing your phone number on our website forms or booking system, you give consent to receive order-related phone calls and text messages (SMS) regarding your dumpster rental dispatch, status, and service updates. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared, sold, or transferred to any third party. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only to vetted third-party vendors and service providers who help us operate our website, process payments, host data, or service customer accounts, strictly bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels to analyze user interaction, remember quote preferences, and measure conversion data from advertising campaigns (such as Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising). You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when cookies are placed.

7. Data Security

We utilize commercial-grade security protocols, including Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/HTTPS) encryption, firewalls, and restricted administrative access to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your information from our active records. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
