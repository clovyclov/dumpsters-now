Estimating Debris Weight for Plymouth Kitchen & Bath Projects

Demolition waste from kitchens and bathrooms is significantly heavier than general household clutter because of high-density materials:

1. Cabinetry & Countertops

  • Laminate & Wood Cabinets: A standard 10-foot kitchen cabinet run weighs approximately 250 – 400 lbs.
  • Granite & Quartz Countertops: Heavy stone countertops weigh roughly 18 – 25 lbs per square foot. A typical 50 sq. ft. kitchen counter section can weigh over 1,000 lbs.

2. Tile, Drywall & Plaster (Historic Plymouth Homes)

  • Older homes near Downtown Plymouth and Old Village frequently feature lathe and plaster walls, which weigh up to 8 – 10 lbs per square foot (nearly double modern drywall at 1.6 lbs/sq. ft.).
  • Floor and wall ceramic/porcelain tile weighs between 4 – 6 lbs per square foot. A typical 80 sq. ft. bathroom floor and shower tear-out yields over 500 lbs of heavy rubble.

100% Driveway Protection for Plymouth Residential Properties

Plymouth properties feature high-value decorative concrete, stamped brick pavers, and asphalt driveways. Placing a steel roll-off container directly onto these surfaces without precautions risks scratching, cracking, or gouging.

At Dumpsters Now, every delivery includes our 100% Driveway Protection System:

  1. Rubber-Wheeled Containers: Our specialized roll-off fleet utilizes heavy-duty rubber wheels that roll smoothly across driveways rather than scraping bare steel skids.
  2. Heavy-Duty Wood Board Placement: Prior to setting down the dumpster, our drivers place thick wooden boards beneath contact points to distribute weight evenly and prevent surface damage.

Local Plymouth Placement & Ordinance Guidelines

  • Private Property Placement: Placing a dumpster on your private driveway in Plymouth City or Plymouth Township requires no local municipal permits.
  • Historic Neighborhood Access: In areas with narrow driveways or tight street turns (like Old Village or near Kellogg Park), our compact 15-yard and 20-yard dumpsters are specially engineered to fit neatly inside standard single-car driveway footprints without blocking sidewalks or garage access.
