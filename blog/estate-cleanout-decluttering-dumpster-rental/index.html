The Ultimate Estate Cleanout & Decluttering Guide for Michigan Homeowners

Home Cleanouts • August 5, 2026 • By Hysam Hammad (Owner & Operations Director)

Tackling an estate cleanout or whole-house decluttering project can feel overwhelming. Whether you are managing the estate of a loved one, preparing a family home in Northville for sale, or clearing out years of accumulated clutter from a basement in Bloomfield Hills, the sheer volume of items to process can quickly stall momentum.

Having executed hundreds of estate cleanouts across Metro Detroit, the team at Dumpsters Now compiled this practical checklist to help you stay organized, save time, and execute a stress-free cleanout.

Step 1: Secure Important Documents & Valuables First

Before bringing in a dumpster or hiring estate liquidators, walk through the property to collect and safeguard essential items:

  • Legal & Financial Records: Birth certificates, property deeds, tax documents, stock certificates, and military discharge papers.
  • Family Keepsakes: Photo albums, family heirlooms, letters, and jewelry.
  • Hidden Cash & Collectibles: Check drawer bottoms, suit pockets, books, and safe boxes—older generations often stored cash or jewelry in unexpected household spots.

Step 2: Establish a 4-Zone Sorting System

To keep your workflow moving smoothly, set up designated staging zones in the driveway or garage:

  1. Keep / Family Distribution: Items designated for family members to keep.
  2. Sell / Estate Sale: High-value furniture, antiques, tools, and vintage items.
  3. Donate: Usable clothing, books, kitchenware, and furniture in good condition (consider local Metro Detroit charities like Purple Heart or Habitat ReStore).
  4. Disposal / Roll-Off Dumpster: Broken furniture, worn mattresses, old carpeting, trash, and non-recyclable household clutter.

Step 3: Choose the Right Dumpster Size for an Estate Purge

Choosing the right roll-off container size is critical for estate cleanouts because bulky items like sofas, mattresses, and wall units take up significant volume:

  • 20-Yard Dumpster (Starts at $425): Ideal for 2-3 bedroom home cleanouts, partial estate purges, or garage + attic combinations.
  • 30-Yard Dumpster (Starts at $465): Recommended for whole-home estate cleanouts (4+ bedrooms), hoarding situations, or properties with heavy furniture, carpeting, and basement storage built up over decades.
  • 40-Yard Dumpster (Starts at $550): Necessary for large estate properties, commercial cleanouts, or multi-building barn/shed purges.

Step 4: What Can & Cannot Go in Your Dumpster?

Our roll-off dumpsters accommodate almost all standard household items, including:

  • Accepted Items: Furniture, mattresses, carpets, dry drywall, wood trim, yard debris, clothing, cardboard, appliances (without Freon), and general trash.
  • Prohibited Items: Wet paint, motor oil, car batteries, propane tanks, asbestos, and hazardous chemicals (contact your local county household hazardous waste division for disposal of these materials).
