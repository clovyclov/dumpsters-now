Waste Loading Guidelines

Approved Debris vs. Restricted Waste Guidelines

Dumpsters Now makes waste disposal simple, safe, and transparent. Check our acceptable materials list below to ensure a seamless rental from drop-off to final haul-away.

✔ Accepted Waste Materials

  • Household furniture, mattresses, couches, tables
  • Drywall, lumber, studs, flooring, carpet & tile
  • Roofing shingles, underlayment & flashing
  • Tree branches, brush, sod, leaves & yard waste
  • Concrete, asphalt, brick & stone (heavy load capacity)

✖ Prohibited Items

  • Liquid paint, solvents & toxic chemicals
  • Car batteries, motor oil & gasoline
  • Asbestos & biohazardous waste
  • Propane tanks & pressurized cylinders
  • Freon appliances (unless certified drained)
