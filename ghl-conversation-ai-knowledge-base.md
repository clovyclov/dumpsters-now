## 5. Disposal Guidelines: Allowed vs. Prohibited Debris

### Allowed Waste Materials
- **Household Debris:** Furniture, couches, tables, dressers, mattresses, box springs, desks, books, toys, clothing, general household junk.
- **Construction & Remodeling:** Drywall, drywall joint compound, wood framing, 2x4s, subflooring, tile, ceramic, siding, cabinets, countertops, sinks, PVC pipes, doors, windows, scrap metals.
- **Roofing Materials:** Asphalt shingles, tar paper, plywood sheathing, underlayment, flashing (recommended in 15 or 20-yard bins for weight distribution).
- **Yard Debris:** Tree branches, brush, sod, bushes, logs, dirt (in moderate volumes), lawn clippings.

### Prohibited Materials (Michigan DEQ Regulations)
- Liquid paints, lacquers, stains, and thinners
- Solvents, motor oil, gasoline, diesel, antifreeze, transmission fluid
- Automotive batteries
- Propane tanks and pressurized gas cylinders
- Asbestos materials
- Biohazardous materials, medical waste, infectious waste
- Industrial toxic chemicals and hazardous drums

### Items Requiring Special Handling / Extra Disposal Fees
- **Refrigerators, Freezers, Air Conditioners:** Prohibited unless certified and tagged as drained of freon/refrigerant by a licensed HVAC technician.
- **Tires:** Landfills charge a dedicated disposal fee per tire. Notify dispatch before loading tires.

## 6. Weight Verification & Overage Billing
- Disposal trucks drive onto certified state-regulated scales at the disposal transfer station upon arrival (gross weight).
- After emptying the dumpster, the truck drives back onto the scale (tare weight).
- The difference is the net debris weight printed on the certified scale receipt.
- If waste exceeds the weight allowance included in the flat rate (e.g., 2 tons for 15-yard, 3 tons for 20-yard), overage is billed per additional ton at the prevailing transfer station rate.
